You are making a set of small food and product images (sprites) for a fun app called Swiggy Pile.

In the app, every one of these items lies in a big pile at the bottom of a black screen. When the user types a word like "idli" into the search bar, all the idlis jump out of the pile with real physics and line up in a neat block, with a count like "× 42 · ₹4,100". The images will be cut out and dropped onto a black background, so clean edges matter more than anything else.

YOUR JOB
Generate one image for every item in the list below, one at a time, in the order given. Make them all in the exact same style so they look like one matching set. When you hand each image back, name it with the file name shown.

IMAGE RULES (apply to every single image)
1. Transparent PNG. The background must be fully transparent: no white, no colour, no gradient.
2. One object only, centred, filling about 80% of the frame.
3. Square, 1024 × 1024 pixels.
4. No drop shadow, no ground or floor, no reflection, no surface under the object. The app adds its own physics, so a shadow would make the item look like it is floating.
5. No text, no brand names, no logos anywhere, including on the packets, cans and bottles.
6. Show the whole object. Nothing cropped at the edges.
7. It must read clearly at a very small size (about 40–60 pixels on screen), so use bold, simple shapes and clear colours, without fussy detail.
8. It must look good on a black background. Avoid dark items with no outline that would disappear into black.

STYLE (identical for every image)
Soft, glossy 3D toy-like render, slightly playful and cute, three-quarter front view, bright natural colours, soft even studio lighting, smooth surfaces. Treat all the items as if they belong to the same toy collection.
